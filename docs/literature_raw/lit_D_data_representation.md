## 1. Data: what makes a world model plannable

### 1a. Counterfactual / off-support action accuracy and data
1. VERIFIED. FIRM-WM (2609.22816). Y. Wu ... A. Zhang. Sep 2026, cs.LG. ~3M-param pixel WM; "factual" broad trajectories + "common-reset intervention branches" (reset env to recorded state, try alternative actions); goal-comparable typed state vs 128-d dynamic part. TwoRoom 99.0, Reacher 92.7, OGBench-Cube 88.0 vs LeWM 89.0/88.0/70.0. Code none found. Relevance: CLOSEST competitor to our "decorrelate actions from states" result (interventional branches from same state). Note it needs resettable simulator. Threat to novelty on the data axis; our Push-T 41->72 and counterfactual 3.9-20x numbers need to be positioned against it.
2. VERIFIED. On the Identifiability of Controlled World Models (2607.22430). X. Zhang ... S. E. Li. Jul 2026, cs.LG. Joint identifiability: representation (spectral separation) + transition (conditional action variation); counterfactual error bounded by inverse of weakest action-excitation margin; LeJEPA-style objective; 4 nonlinear observation settings (synthetic). Code none. Relevance: theoretical backing for why expert data (low action variation given state) fails; cite as theory, we supply the planning-benchmark evidence.
3. VERIFIED. Do Robotic World Models Really Follow Actions? (WorldEcho / WorldSync) (2608.24885). S. Chen ... S. Zhang. Aug 2026. Benchmark of off-expert action following; WorldSync = broader action coverage + Action-Forcing Expert + intervention-consistency alignment. RoboTwin + real robot; (via search snippet, UNVERIFIED number) 65% vs 56-57% policy success after 2 rounds. Code none found. Relevance: same diagnosis (expert-only data) in video-WM land.
4. VERIFIED. PlayWorld: Learning Robot World Models from Autonomous Play (2603.09030). T. Yin ... A. Majumdar. Mar 2026 (v3 Apr). Autonomous self-play data for video WMs; up to 40% gain over human data on policy evaluation, RL in WM gives +65% real success. Project: robot-playworld.github.io. Relevance: the "play data" argument at the video-WM scale; no latent-planning/ JEPA analysis, no data-mixing study.
5. VERIFIED. World Models Dream of Success: Diagnosing and Repairing Failure Insensitivity in Robot World Models (CureWM) (2610.09134). J. Xu ... Y. Shi. Oct 6 2026, cs.RO. Success-like prediction on failures 80% -> 30-43% (LIBERO, 484 counterfactual failures); counterfactual failures give success-failure value gap 0.124 vs 0.014 for on-policy failures. Code github.com/jiuyixu25/CureWM. Relevance: data-source ablation (counterfactual replay >> on-policy); same message as ours.
6. VERIFIED. MiraBench: Evaluating Action-Conditioned Reliability in Robotic World Models (2605.29360). T. Yang ... Y. Yang. May 2026. 16k human judgments, 12 configs; physics, action-following, optimism bias. Relevance: evaluation protocols for counterfactual fidelity.
7. VERIFIED. Overcoming Statistical Bias in Action-Controllable World Models (CoCo) (2608.04653). Y. Shi ... J. Fu. Aug 2026, cs.CV. Counterfactual consistency losses (inverse-action, zero-action rollouts, mirrored scenes); ARC/DE metrics, Mini-SSMB; VP2 planning 73.1% avg. Code none found. Relevance: loss-side fix for visual-inertia shortcut; video model, not frozen-encoder JEPA.
8. VERIFIED. Action-Consequence Alignment for Reliable Planning and Self-Improving in Latent World Models (ACA) (2610.04539). J. Wang ... L. Shao. Oct 3 2026, cs.AI. Penalizes nearby counterfactual actions that get lower prediction error than the factual one; "ACA-guided data collection beats random local sampling". Code claimed github.com/smithgun2005/ACA (from search page, not on abs). Relevance: closest to "counterfactual loss + data collection" for latent WMs; abstract has no numbers (I did not read the full text).
9. VERIFIED. One-Step Prediction Fidelity Is a Poor Proxy for Planning Performance in JEPA World Models (2610.19375). J. Cheung, Z. He. Oct 14 2026. Fidelity-planning r = -0.010 across 16 checkpoints; action-swap raises pred error 29.1x PushT / 15.7x TwoRoom / 2.1x Cube (via search snippet). Relevance: Cube's low action-sensitivity (2.1x) matches our "no gain on Cube"; supports using action-sensitivity as the diagnostic. Very fresh; cite.
10. VERIFIED. World Model Control by Trajectory Reachability Metrics (TRM) (2605.22164). L. Li ... Q. Liu. May 2026 (v2 Aug). LeWM TwoRoom 7.0% -> 96.7% with a temporal reachability metric; PLDM 32.7 -> 84.0; "balanced coverage" fixes a 0% doorway-coverage failure to 100%. Relevance: explicit coverage-vs-planning evidence on TwoRoom/PLDM; shows data coverage and cost metric interact.
11. VERIFIED. Learning from Reward-Free Offline Data: A Case for Planning with Latent Dynamics Models (PLDM) (2502.14819). V. Sobal ... Y. LeCun. Feb 2025 (v4 Oct 2025). Latent planning beats model-free RL on suboptimal/random data, generalizes to unseen layouts, good at stitching. Project latent-planning.github.io. Relevance: the standard citation for "random/suboptimal data suffices", which our expert-data result nuances.
12. VERIFIED. Learning Latent Plans from Play (1903.01973). C. Lynch ... P. Sermanet. CoRL 2019. Play data baseline reference.
13. (search-only, UNVERIFIED by abs fetch) Planning to Explore via Self-Supervised World Models (Plan2Explore), Sekar et al., ICML 2020 (PMLR v119). Disagreement-driven exploration; classical exploration-for-WM reference. Not applied to frozen-encoder JEPA WMs as far as found.

### 1b. Action-sensitivity / inverse-dynamics / auxiliary-loss line (the "losses" fix)

## 3. Saturation judgement (Oct 2026)

SATURATING FAST (avoid as headline): auxiliary losses to make LeWM/JEPA latents action-sensitive or plan-aligned. Within ~3 months there are AD-WM, Delta-JEPA, 2609.03565, D-JEPA, DA-LeWM, RC-aux, Temporal-Distance JEPA, AnisoWM, Hamiltonian JEPA, SCALE, ProWorld, SLIM-IDM, ACA. Everyone reports gains on TwoRoom/PushT/Cube/Reacher with the LeWM codebase; Cube is the contested benchmark (LeWM 64-79, 87-92 for the best). A new loss alone is a crowded CVPR submission.

CROWDED BUT NOT CLOSED: counterfactual/interventional data for diffusion/video WMs (WorldSync, CureWM, PlayWorld, MiraBench) and FIRM-WM/ACA for latent WMs. Gap that remains: a controlled, benchmark-style study of DATA properties for frozen-encoder latent planners (state-action decorrelation, action coverage, expert vs random vs mixed ratios, support of the planner's CEM candidates) with a quantitative law linking data statistic -> counterfactual error -> planning success. Only 2607.22430 (theory, synthetic), TRM (coverage case study), and 2610.19375 (one-step-error proxy) touch this. This is the best single-GPU opportunity: cheap (Push-T/Wall/TwoRoom train in hours), no robot needed, and our 3.9-20x counterfactual / 41->72 PushT result is already a data point. Risks: FIRM-WM (interventions from simulator reset) and ACA (data collection guided by counterfactual loss) are the direct competitors; position as "what data statistic predicts plannability and a recipe that needs no reset", and include Cube honestly (the null there is informative: 2610.19375 shows action sensitivity only 2.1x on Cube, so Cube failure is representation/cost, not data).

OPEN: encoder choice for frozen-feature planning on SMALL objects. Evidence is thin: JEPA-WMs (DINO > V-JEPA), DUET-DINO (DINOv3 > V-JEPA 2), Better Slots (slot metrics fail for small Cube objects), SLIM (end-to-end collapse). No paper found that (a) compares DINOv2 vs DINOv3 vs SigLIP 2 vs V-JEPA 2.1 vs SAM-features under one planner with a latent-cost ranking metric, (b) studies resolution / patch-size / crop-to-workspace effects on goal-cost ranking, or (c) fine-tunes/gram-anchors a task-aware head on frozen patches. Our Metaworld push 8% (latent cost) vs 83% (true cost) and Cube Spearman 0.24 are exactly the kind of measurement that is missing; DA-LeWM's Plan-Real Spearman is the only comparable metric (end-to-end model). A single-GPU group can do this: encoder sweep + cost-ranking Spearman + a light probe/learned cost head or object-mask-pooled feature cost. Likelihood of overlap: moderate (Better Slots / TC-WM / SLIM authors could extend), so move fast.

OPEN BUT HARD: SAM-based masks as WM state/cost (only Mask2Real-WM, FOCUS); needs mask tracking and is a heavier pipeline, but a "mask-pooled DINO feature cost" is a cheap, publishable variant.

Suggested CVPR-level contribution (single GPU): (1) data-statistics study for plannability with counterfactual-error metric + a reset-free decorrelation/augmentation recipe; (2) frozen-encoder cost-ranking benchmark (encoder x resolution x pooling x object-mask pooling) with Spearman/regret diagnostics and a task-aware cost head; combine as "what the data and the features must provide for latent planning". Compare against AD-WM and FIRM-WM numbers on Cube, and against LeWM/DINO-WM.
