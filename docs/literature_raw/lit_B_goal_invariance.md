## A. Papers named in internal notes (all 7 VERIFIED, IDs and titles correct)

1. **Supervise What Decides Success: Criterion-Aligned Auxiliary Losses for Latent World-Model Planning** - Takumi Hara, Kanata Suzuki. arXiv 2610.01224 (1 Oct 2026). VERIFIED. Claims end-effector position is encoded with more error than the success criterion tolerates in 4 latent WMs; adds a linear-head auxiliary regression of success-criterion quantities (+3.5 PushT, +3.4 cube). Goal-side invariance: NOT measured (it is encoder/training-side). Code: none on page. Relation: supports "success criterion != latent distance"; a competitor for the "learned task-aware representation" method (supervised by success quantities, same spirit), but it does not touch goal-image variation.
2. **When Low Prediction Error Misleads Planning: Diagnosing Representation, Dynamics, and Decision Failures in Latent World Models** - Rui Min, Jing Du. 2610.05550 (4 Oct 2026). VERIFIED. Candidate-pool center vs action-dependent error decomposition; 4 models x 4 tasks; LeWM study with oracle actions; notes "render-matched goal" on Reacher and that Cube's encoded-goal cost "stays uninformative"; physical Spearman of ranking. Goal-side nuisance: touches goal rendering (render-matched goal) but abstract does not frame it as an invariance requirement. Relation: closest on "ranking diagnostics" (Spearman/top-k elite); our 36-43% flip metric should be positioned against it. Code: none.
3. **CF-JEPA: Improving Robustness of JEPA World Models via Controllability Factorization** - Morgan Byrd, Sehoon Ha. 2610.00727 (30 Sep 2026). VERIFIED. Splits latent into controllable/uncontrollable subspaces; robust to distractors; LeWM PushT nominal 0.96 vs CF-JEPA 0.78 nominal / 0.87 w/ distractors (numbers from a secondary search snippet, UNVERIFIED in table). Goal-side: distractors are observation-side; the abstract does not say goals are perturbed separately. Code: project page https://morganbyrd03.github.io/cf-jepa/. Relation: supports "uncontrollable factors should not enter the cost", but note that for our drawer/button results the *arm* is controllable yet goal-irrelevant, which controllability factorization would NOT remove (key differentiator: relevance != controllability; see Denoised MDP's 4-way taxonomy).
4. **JEPA-Bisim: Learning Robust Visual Representations for Planning with Joint-Embedding Predictive World Models** (v1 title: "Learning Invariant Visual Representations for Planning with JEPA World Models") - Leonardo Toso ... James Anderson. 2602.18639 (Feb 2026, v2 1 Oct 2026). VERIFIED. Bisimulation encoder on DINO-WM; PointMaze and PushT under test-time background change/distractors; latent up to 10x smaller; DINOv2/SimDINOv2/iBOT. Goal-side: robustness is evaluated on observation shifts; not an explicit goal-equivalence study. Code: none on page. Relation: nearest prior *method* for invariance via bisimulation in JEPA-WMs; threatens novelty of "learned invariant representation" but bisimulation is reward/dynamics-defined and does not define goal-image equivalence.
5. **Goal Sets, Not Goal States: Queryable Robot Goals through Goal-Set Hindsight Relabeling (GS-HER)** - Carlos Velez Garcia, Jorge Pomares. 2606.09476 (8 Jun 2026). VERIFIED. Hindsight relabeling to goal SETS defined by a binary query over which variables define success; OGBench, 5 offline learners; helps "when nuisance dimensions hurt full-state goals"; one checkpoint serves multiple goal predicates. Goal-side nuisance: YES (state-vector goals, not images). Code: none on page. Relation: strongest conceptual neighbour of "task equivalence": it states that exact full-state goals overconstrain. State-based, offline GCRL, not frozen-encoder image WMs with CEM. Must be cited and distinguished.
6. **Temporal-Distance JEPA: Plan-Aware Representation Learning for Latent World Model Predictive Control (TD-JEPA)** - Jiaxin Bai, Jiaxuan Xiong. 2607.25337 (28 Jul 2026). VERIFIED. Directed temporal cost on LeWM backbone; Two-Room 100.0 vs 97.4, OGB-Cube +14.2 over LeWM under Euclidean, Push-T gains. Goal-side nuisance: not measured. Code: https://github.com/HKBU-KnowComp/Temporal-Distance-JEPA. Relation: a learned goal-cost baseline we should compare against; a temporal-distance cost is in principle partly invariant to irrelevant factors, but untested.
7. **Keeping JEPA World Models Plannable When Little of the Frame Moves** - Florian Strohm ... Marco Huber. 2610.03137 (2 Oct 2026). VERIFIED. SLIM benchmark (small objects, paired visual and language goals); LeWM <1% success; inverse-dynamics aux loss 0.003 -> 0.35; action-sensitivity probe; language-goal head. Goal-side invariance: no. Relation: shows the failure of LeWM is encoder action-insensitivity, a different failure mode from ours; useful to separate "cost insensitive" from "cost sensitive to the wrong thing".

## B. Closest competitors found in this search (new, all VERIFIED unless noted)

8. **Better Slots, Better Worlds: Representation Quality & Robustness in Object-Centric World Models** - Shukrullo Nazirjonov ... Georg Martius. 2608.12078 (12 Aug 2026). VERIFIED. Controlled shift study on PushT (LeWM, DINO-WM, SlotContrast-WM). Table lists **goal.color** (ID light green; OOD red/blue/black) as a perturbed factor, and background colour is "most damaging for LeWM". The text I could access gave NO numeric goal-colour-only result and did not clearly separate goal-only from observation shifts (numbers are in Figure 3 only). Goal-side nuisance: YES, but as one factor among a shift grid. HIGHEST-PRIORITY ADVERSARIAL ITEM: it likely already shows that colour/appearance shifts collapse LeWM on PushT, which overlaps the "recolouring drops 90% -> 7%" finding. Read Fig. 3 and appendix before claiming novelty of that number. Code: none on page.
9. **The Planning Limits of Latent World Models** - Ali Alrasheed ... Naveed Akhtar. 2609.39235 (30 Sep 2026). VERIFIED. 5 frozen SSL backbones, Meta-World + BridgeData V2; ranking works only 5-10 steps ahead; 92% -> 41% with distance. Search-summary (unverified in text) says a "goal from another reset" variant gives 7 tasks better / 5 worse. Goal-side: goal-distance and goal-instance variation, not nuisance. Uses Meta-World (same benchmark as ours). Relation: supports that the goal image choice changes outcomes; must be cited. Code: none on page.
10. **Decision-Metric Alignment in Latent World Models: Diagnostics and Action-Conditioned Objectives for MPC Planning** - Jiawei Wang, Minglei Li. 2608.18746 (19 Aug 2026). VERIFIED. 11. **Diagnosing JEPA World Models with Action-Conditioned Predictive Consistency** - Guo An ... Qi Tian. 2608.12939 (13 Aug 2026). VERIFIED. "Invariance Radius" = drift of clean vs visually-altered histories rolled out under same actions; "Separation Rate". Observation-side invariance, not goal-side. Relation: gives a ready vocabulary/metric for invariance of rollouts; we need an analogue for goal-image invariance. Code: none.
12. **Representation Geometry Matters for Planning with JEPA World Models (SCALE)** - Jiaming Hu ... Qingjun Cui. 2608.16287 (17 Aug 2026; v3 6 Oct). VERIFIED. Relation: the closest existing *method* to "learn a task-aware goal-cost geometry" (supervised by privileged task-relevant state). 13. **Anisotropic Representations Improve Planning in JEPA World Models (AnisoWM)** - Mingu Kang ... Joonseok Lee. 2609.37441 (29 Sep 2026). VERIFIED. 14. **Value-guided action planning with JEPA world models** - Matthieu Destrade ... Yann LeCun. 2601.00844 (28 Dec 2025). VERIFIED. Shapes embedding distances to approximate a goal-conditioned value. Goal-side nuisance not studied. Relation: learned goal-conditioned cost baseline.
15. **Traj-LeWM: Path-Aware World-Model Planning via Latent Trajectory Cost** - Xiaodi Huang, Tao Huang. 2608.14125 (14 Aug 2026). VERIFIED. Learned goal-conditioned trajectory cost from preferences; +3 Push-T, +14 OGBench-Cube. No nuisance study.
16. **D-JEPA: A Decision-Aligned Latent World Model** - Shuaijun Liu ... Ningxin Su. 2609.24749 (21 Sep 2026). VERIFIED. Learns ranking from executed outcomes; 87.89% PushT. Project site https://nebulis-lab.com/D-JEPA.

## C. Goal-conditioned RL / temporal-distance / goal representation (classic and recent)

32. **Dual Goal Representations** - Seohong Park, ... Sergey Levine. 2510.06714, ICLR 2026. VERIFIED. State represented by temporal distances to all states; provably depends only on dynamics, retains optimal-policy info and discards **exogenous noise**; OGBench 20 tasks. Goal-side nuisance: YES in theory (invariance to exogenous noise), not controllable-but-irrelevant factors (an arm pose that does not affect outcome). Its principle (equivalence defined by temporal distance to all states) is the cleanest formal precedent of "goal representation defined by what matters for reaching" and is the biggest conceptual threat to a pure-theory novelty claim; our contribution would be (a) goal images in frozen-encoder WM planners, (b) the controllable-but-irrelevant case, (c) the measurement. Code: none on page.
33. **Offline Goal-conditioned RL with Quasimetric Representations (TMD)** - Vivek Myers, ... Sergey Levine. 2509.20478. VERIFIED (no venue on page). Quasimetric + action invariance; noisy/visual stitching. Code via tmd-website.github.io (search result, not fetched).
34. **Optimal Goal-Reaching RL via Quasimetric Learning (QRL)** - Tongzhou Wang, ... Amy Zhang. 2304.01203, ICML 2023. VERIFIED.
35. **Bisimulation Makes Analogies in Goal-Conditioned RL** - Philippe Hansen-Estruch, ... Sergey Levine. 2204.13060, ICML 2022. VERIFIED. 36. **Learning Transferable Skills using Goal-Conditioned Bisimulation** - M. A. Abbasfar, ... M. H. Rohban. 2610.00676 (30 Sep 2026). VERIFIED. Skills depend only on state features affecting execution; layout transfer. Marginal.

## Findings

### Closest prior work to "task equivalence as a required invariance of goal costs"
1. Dual Goal Representations (2510.06714): invariance of the goal representation to exogenous noise, derived from dynamics. Conceptually the same requirement, state/pixel-agnostic, but only for exogenous (uncontrollable) noise and in offline GCRL, not frozen-encoder WM+CEM.
2. GS-HER (2606.09476): explicitly says exact-state goals overconstrain; goal sets defined by which variables define success. Same insight on the data/relabeling side; state vectors, no WM planning.
3. Goal-conditioned bisimulation (2204.13060): equivalence over (state, goal) tasks.
4. Better Slots, Better Worlds (2608.12078): perturbs goal colour/background on PushT with LeWM/DINO-WM; likely overlaps our recolour experiment (numbers only in a figure; check).
5. SCALE (2608.16287), SpecWM (2610.15429), AnisoWM, Decision-Metric Alignment: all argue latent Euclidean goal distance is not the task cost and fix it on the encoder side with privileged targets.
6. RAC (2107.09047), XIRL, ROLL, Dynamic Object Masks: agent/embodiment/object-level removal in costs or goals.

### Novelty verdict (abstract-level; confirm with full-text reads)
- NOT novel alone: "goal images contain nuisance; Euclidean latent distance is a poor cost; learn an invariant/task-aware representation" (Dual GR, DBC, TIA, Denoised MDP, JEPA-Bisim, CF-JEPA, SCALE, ...).
- No paper found that (a) holds the task outcome fixed and varies only the *agent/arm pose* (controllable but irrelevant) in the goal image, (b) measures candidate-ranking flips, chosen plans and closed-loop success under that change for frozen-encoder JEPA WMs, and (c) shows irrelevance is task-dependent (Push-T pusher matters, drawer arm irrelevant, button arm required). This combination appears unclaimed as of the searches (5 targeted adversarial queries; absence of evidence, search tool is not exhaustive).
- Position the claim as: "task-equivalence violation" is a *measurable failure of goal costs*, with a task-dependent equivalence relation, not a universal denoising. Dual GR + GS-HER are the must-cite theory/precedent; Better Slots is the must-check empirical overlap; Planning Limits (also Meta-World) is the must-check for the Meta-World goal-instance angle.
- Risks: (i) 2608.12078 may already report goal-colour collapse on LeWM PushT; (ii) reviewers will ask why not mask the robot (Mirage/Cloak/RAC style) or use mask goals (ROLL, Dynamic Object Masks) - but a mask needs to know which factors are irrelevant, which our task-dependence result argues against; (iii) CF-JEPA/Denoised-MDP-style controllability-based removal would delete the arm in the button task where it matters, and keep it in drawer where it doesn't - a good ablation.

### Open gaps (supports a method)
- Goal-side evaluation protocol for JEPA/DINO WMs (goal-only perturbations, equivalence-preserving edits) is absent; Invariance Radius (2608.12939) is observation-side only.
- No method learns the many-to-one *equivalence class of goal images* per task from data (e.g., from success labels or hindsight sets) and uses it as the CEM cost on frozen encoders; closest: SCALE (privileged state), Dual GR (temporal distance), GS-HER (query sets), VIP/XIRL (learned goal distance). Cheap baselines to include: arm-masked goal (Mirage-style inpaint), DINO patch-subset cost, TD-JEPA/Value-guided cost, SCALE, Dual-GR-style embedding.
- Task-dependent relevance (which factors matter) is only gestured at by DRPE (2609.32322) in gridworlds.
