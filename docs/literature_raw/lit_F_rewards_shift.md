## A. Learned rewards / values / progress from pretrained visual and foundation models
1. VIP: Towards Universal Visual Reward and Representation via Value-Implicit Pre-Training. Y. J. Ma ... A. Zhang. arXiv 2210.00030, ICLR 2023 (spotlight). VERIFIED. Goal-conditioned value function learned from Ego4D; embedding distance to goal as dense reward; few-shot offline RL with 20 trajectories. Code: not on abs page (facebookresearch/vip is the known repo, UNVERIFIED). Relevance: the canonical "value-shaped embedding" - latent distance becomes monotone in time-to-goal. Addresses (a) in principle (fix the cost, not the encoder); not tested on small objects.
2. LIV: Language-Image Representations and Rewards for Robotic Control. Y. J. Ma ... D. Jayaraman. arXiv 2306.00958, ICML 2023. VERIFIED. Extends VIP with language-image contrastive; reward = goal-embedding progress. (a) partially.
3. R3M: A Universal Visual Representation for Robot Manipulation. S. Nair ... A. Gupta. arXiv 2203.12601, CoRL 2022. VERIFIED. Time-contrastive + language; +20% over scratch on 12 sim tasks. Baseline encoder for progress costs.
4. Where are we in the search for an Artificial Visual Cortex for Embodied Intelligence? (VC-1/CortexBench). A. Majumdar ... F. Meier. arXiv 2303.18240, NeurIPS 2023 (venue not on abs page, UNVERIFIED). VERIFIED ID. 17 tasks; no single encoder dominates - supports "frozen-encoder cost is task dependent".
5. Vision Language Models are In-Context Value Learners (GVL). Y. J. Ma ... F. Xia. arXiv 2411.04549, preprint (ICLR 2025 UNVERIFIED). VERIFIED. Zero-shot per-frame progress via shuffled-frame VLM prompting, 300+ tasks. (a): candidate progress oracle for puck; not fine-grained at cm scale (untested).
6. RoboCLIP: One Demonstration is Enough to Learn Robot Policies. S. A. Sontakke ... L. Itti. arXiv 2310.07899 (NeurIPS 2023 UNVERIFIED). VERIFIED. Video-language similarity as sparse-ish episode reward; 2-3x zero-shot over IL baselines. Weak for dense progress.
7. Eureka: Human-Level Reward Design via Coding Large Language Models. Y. J. Ma ... A. Anandkumar. arXiv 2310.12931, ICLR 2024. VERIFIED. LLM-written reward code; beats human rewards on 83% of 29 tasks. Needs state access, so applicable only if puck pose is available in sim; useful as upper-bound/oracle shaping, not for pixel-only planning.
8. TimeRewarder: Learning Dense Reward from Passive Videos via Frame-wise Temporal Distance. Y. Liu ... Y. Gao. arXiv 2509.26627, ICML 2026 spotlight. VERIFIED. Predicts temporal distance between frame pairs as dense reward; near-perfect on 9/10 Meta-World tasks with 200k interactions. Directly on Metaworld; strongest match to (a) as a drop-in progress cost. Code: not on abs page.
9. RynnValue: Scaling Robotic Value Foundation Models with Temporal Distance. D. Huang ... X. Li. arXiv 2608.09853, preprint. VERIFIED. Cost-to-go from timestamps, 7k hours/3M clips; Kendall tau_a 0.704 (v2 abs page; the search snippet showed 0.675 = v1, so numbers changed between versions); inference-time value guidance 67.5 -> 80.0. Code: abstract says open-source, GitHub alibaba-damo-academy/RynnValue (seen in search, not fetched). (a) as pretrained VLM progress cost; heavy (8B).
10. Large Reward Models: Generalizable Online Robot Reward Generation with VLMs. Y. Wu ... Y. Wang. arXiv 2603.16065, preprint. VERIFIED. Progress + completion + temporal-contrastive (pairwise ranking) rewards. Pairwise ranking reward is a model for ranking small pushes.
11. TOPReward: Token Probabilities as Hidden Zero-Shot Rewards for Robotics. S. Chen ... R. Krishna. arXiv 2602.19313. VERIFIED. Training-free VLM token-likelihood progress; ManiRewardBench 130 tasks.
12. ARM: Advantage Reward Modeling for Long-Horizon Manipulation. Y. Mao ... H. Chen. arXiv 2604.03037. VERIFIED. Relative advantage (progressive/regressive/stagnant) instead of absolute progress - matches "ranks small pushes weakly" issue.
13. ReWiND: Language-Guided Rewards Teach Robot Policies without New Demonstrations. J. Zhang ... J. Zhang. arXiv 2505.10911, CoRL 2025 oral. VERIFIED. Video-rewind augmentation to learn progress reward on Metaworld-style tasks; up to 2.4x reward generalization.
14. Video Prediction Models as Rewards for RL (VIPER). A. Escontrela ... P. Abbeel. arXiv 2305.14343. VERIFIED (title/authors only). Log-likelihood of next frame under video model as reward.
15. Progress Reward Modeling for Robotic Learning: A Comprehensive Survey. J. Zhang ... H. Liu. arXiv 2607.21655. VERIFIED. Good citation hook / taxonomy.
16. Optimal Goal-Reaching RL via Quasimetric Learning (QRL). T. Wang ... A. Zhang. arXiv 2304.01203, ICML 2023. VERIFIED. Quasimetric distance, optimal under asymmetry; template for replacing Euclidean latent cost.
17. Dual Goal Representations. S. Park, S. Levine. arXiv 2510.06714, ICLR 2026. VERIFIED. State = vector of temporal distances to all states; invariant to raw representation and filters exogenous noise; improves 20 OGBench state/pixel tasks and noisy goals. Closest GCRL answer to (b) (exogenous-noise-invariant goal rep). Code: none listed.
18. HIQL: Offline GCRL with Latent States as Actions. S. Park ... S. Levine. arXiv 2307.11949, NeurIPS 2023. VERIFIED. Hierarchical subgoals as latent actions.

## B. Latent-WM-specific progress costs / cost shaping (most directly competing work)
19. Temporal-Distance JEPA: Plan-Aware Representation Learning for Latent WM MPC. J. Bai, J. Xiong. arXiv 2607.25337. VERIFIED. Directed temporal-progress cost mined from reward-free trajectories on LeWM; Two-Room 100 vs 97.4; OGB-Cube +14.2. Push-T failures with pure temporal-distance cost are almost all post-contact (per snippet) - suggests contact geometry still needs Euclidean term. Code: github.com/HKBU-KnowComp/Temporal-Distance-JEPA. Addresses (a) (progress cost) but not small objects specifically.
20. Traj-LeWM: Path-Aware World-Model Planning via Latent Trajectory Cost. X. Huang, T. Huang. arXiv 2608.14125. VERIFIED. Trajectory-preference supervision + path cost with endpoint distance; +3 PushT, +14 Cube, +7 Reacher, +7 Two-Room. Code none listed. (a) partially; compare to top-k aggregation (c).
21. Latent World Models with Monotone Planning Costs for Image-Goal Navigation. A. Chahe, L. Zhou. arXiv 2608.09073. VERIFIED. Monotone Cost Ranking loss on frozen DINO features + CEM; 2.7x lower orientation error. Direct precedent for your "cost monotonic but weak ranking" finding; fix is a ranking loss.
22. Predictive but Not Plannable: RC-aux for Latent World Models. W. Li, M. Haseyama. arXiv 2605.07278. VERIFIED. Reachability-correction aux on LeWM. Code: github.com/Guang000/RC-aux.
23. Temporal Straightening for Latent Planning. Y. Wang ... M. Ren. arXiv 2603.12231, ICML 2026. VERIFIED. Curvature regularizer making Euclidean latent distance ~geodesic. Code: agenticlearning.ai/temporal-straightening.
24. D-JEPA: A Decision-Aligned Latent World Model. S. Liu, N. Su. arXiv 2609.24749. VERIFIED. Ordinal evidence from executed outcomes; PushT 87.89%, RoboTwin +15.04. Project site only.
25. Decision-Metric Alignment in Latent WMs (DA-LeWM). J. Wang ... M. Li. arXiv 2608.18746. VERIFIED. Plan-Real Spearman / CEM-stage Spearman metrics - reuse as offline ranking metrics matching your offline-ranking vs closed-loop discrepancy (c).
26. Keeping JEPA World Models Plannable When Little of the Frame Moves (SLIM). Strohm ... Huber. arXiv 2610.03137. VERIFIED. See section 0. Strongest direct evidence for (a): encoder latent barely responds to small objects/actions; inverse-dynamics loss fixes it. Threatens novelty of a pure "small-object" claim; differentiate on planning-cost/search side with exact dynamics.
27. Supervise What Decides Success (2610.01224) and When Low Prediction Error Misleads Planning (2610.05550), ARC-Bench (2609.05461): VERIFIED, see section 0. All say encoder/ranking, not dynamics, is the bottleneck; consistent with your exact-dynamics result.

## C. CEM exploration limits, proposals, planners, hierarchy/subgoals
28. Sample-efficient Cross-Entropy Method for Real-time Planning (iCEM). C. Pinneri ... G. Martius. arXiv 2008.06389 (CoRL 2020 UNVERIFIED). VERIFIED. Colored-noise sampling, elite memory; 2.7-22x fewer samples. Colored/temporally correlated noise is a cheap fix for the 4% contact rate.
29. MPPI using Covariance Variable Importance Sampling. G. Williams ... E. Theodorou. arXiv 1509.01149. VERIFIED (ID corresponds to this MPPI paper, not the "information theoretic MPC" paper).
30. SAGE: Subgoal-Conditioned Action Generation for Latent WM Planning. L. Cheng, Y. Wang. arXiv 2607.17973. VERIFIED. Replaces random proposal init with learned subgoal-conditioned generator; PushT@offset150 4.7 -> 64.7; Cube 20.7 -> 67.3. Policy-prior-initialised CEM precedent. Code none.
31. Latent Energy Action Planning with World Models (LEAP). P. Pham, A. Bera. arXiv 2609.03294. VERIFIED. Goal-conditioned proposal init + quasi-Newton on frozen LeWM; 77.5 -> 94.8 avg. Snippet: CEM lowers latent cost while physical divergence grows.
32. Latent Geometry Beyond Search: Amortizing Planning in World Models. H. Nguyen ... X. Huang. arXiv 2605.08732. VERIFIED. GC-IDM replaces CEM, 100-130x cheaper; CEM saturates regardless of budget. Code: github.com/hdnndh/Latent-Geometry-Beyond-Search-Amortizing-Planning-in-World-Models.
33. EpicWorldModel: Exploration-driven Planning with Latent World Models. B. Feng, F. Heide. arXiv 2610.05996, NeurIPS 2026. VERIFIED. Flow-matching predictive variance as exploration bonus in CEM; up to +22% over LeWM. Exploration bonus for CEM; targets occlusion rather than small objects.
34. Sampling-based Kinodynamic Planning with World Models. Y. Gao, Z. Kingston. arXiv 2610.12910, ICRA'27 submission. VERIFIED. RRT/KPIECE in DINO-WM latent; argues CEM/MPPI get trapped; 13.8x faster. Alternative to CEM for sparse contact.
35. Hierarchical Planning with Latent World Models. W. Zhang ... N. Ballas. arXiv 2604.03208, CoRL 2026. VERIFIED. Multi-timescale subgoals; real Franka pick-place 70% vs 0%. Project: kevinghst.github.io/HWM/.
36. Mind the Gap: Promises and Pitfalls of Hierarchical Planning in LeWorldModel (Hi-LeWM). N. Caselli ... S. K. Bhethanabhotla. arXiv 2607.12547, WM@Booth 2026. VERIFIED. Closest hit for "Anchored" waypoint scheme (first waypoint anchored to context end). No paper titled "Anchored Planning" found.
37. H-JEPA (Wancong Zhang, R. Balestriero; 2610.06805, VERIFIED; AntMaze 18->73%), FF-JEPA (S. Masip, T. Tuytelaars; 2606.09311, VERIFIED, no numbers), Dual-WM (D. Zhao, Y. Wang; 2609.37644, VERIFIED; code github.com/DeLin1001/Dual-WM-Official). Hierarchy aids long horizon, not small-object progress.

## D. Small-object-sensitive aggregation / object- and keypoint-centric costs
39. Sparse Planning in Visual World Models via Cost Gradients (CostGrad). Y. Xu, E. Grefenstette. arXiv 2610.10274, NeurIPS 2026. VERIFIED. Selects top-K tokens by gradient norm of planning cost; 50% sparsity matches full on 3/4 benchmarks, 2.6x speedup; evaluated on MetaWorld with CEM (300 cand, 30 iters, 10 elites per snippet). Nearest prior art to top-k patch aggregation (c); note it targets compute, not accuracy. Project page only.
40. Sparse Imagination for Efficient Visual World Model Planning. J. Chun ... T. Kim. arXiv 2506.01392, ICLR 2026. VERIFIED. Random token dropping for rollouts.
41. Better Slots, Better Worlds: Representation Quality & Robustness in Object-Centric World Models. S. Nazirjonov ... G. Martius. arXiv 2608.12078, RLC 2026 workshop. VERIFIED. Slots with good binding more robust than LeWM under shift; DINO-WM competitive; background colour change hurts LeWM most (alters CLS token, per snippet). Addresses (b), and object-centric (a).
42. Point Tracking Improves World Action Models (JOPAT). J. Guan, J. Kannala. arXiv 2605.23856. VERIFIED. Joint latent + 2D track diffusion model (LIBERO). Tracks as motion rep; no track-based goal cost found. Track2Act (H. Bharadhwaj ... S. Tulsiani; 2405.01527, ECCV 2024; VERIFIED) is the point-track goal precedent.
43. No paper found using attention-pooled/GeM/learned patch weighting or point-track distance as a goal COST in latent MPC (several searches). This is a gap -> room for contribution (a).

## Assessment: (a) vs (b)
- (a) small-object / sparse progress: more clearly open at the mechanism level. Encoder-side cause is now published (SLIM 2610.03137, Supervise-What-Decides 2610.01224, 2610.05550, ARC-Bench), and temporal-distance/ranking costs exist (TD-JEPA, Traj-LeWM, Monotone cost). What is NOT published: (i) the exact-dynamics ablation isolating the COST (8% vs 83% true-cost) as the bottleneck independent of dynamics; (ii) the 4%-of-candidates-contact CEM exploration analysis; (iii) any learned patch-weighted/point-track/GeM cost. Risk: reviewers can say SLIM already shows this; the angle must be "even with exact dynamics, latent goal cost + CEM fails," plus a fix (progress-shaped cost + contact-biased proposals e.g. iCEM colored noise / SAGE-style prior). Finding (c) (top-k helped offline, not closed loop) is consistent with ARC-Bench and with exploration being the binding limit; CostGrad is the nearest top-k precedent.
- (b) goal-appearance shift: documented (stable-worldmodel, Better Slots, CF-JEPA, JEPA-Bisim all show LeWM collapsing), and several fixes exist, so the diagnosis alone is less novel; open slice = goal-image-specific shift with a test-time fix (augmentation/invariance, AdaJEPA's stated future work) and DINO-vs-LeWM colour invariance study.
- Recommendation: (a) is the more open and more CVPR-suitable (visual-feature aggregation + progress cost on perception side, clean diagnostic with exact dynamics), provided the contribution includes a working closed-loop fix; (b) is better as a secondary robustness experiment/section.
